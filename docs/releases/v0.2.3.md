## rdpio 0.2.3

Longer battery life on a laptop, with the same picture and the same speed.

### Battery

- **No pictures no one sees.** While the window is minimized, on another
  virtual desktop, the laptop's screen is off or the laptop is locked, the
  remote PC stops sending pictures altogether (RDP and Hyper). When you come
  back, the whole screen is repainted at once.
- **The integrated GPU on battery (RDP).** On a laptop with two GPUs, a
  session on battery decodes and draws on the integrated one, so the
  discrete GPU can switch off. Plugging in or out moves it, with a reconnect
  of a second or two. Hyper already did this.
- **Speakers close after 10 s of silence (RDP)** and open again with the
  next sound, so the audio hardware can sleep.
- **Hyper hands back Wi-Fi and power settings while hidden.** Wi-Fi may nap
  and scan again, and the screen and the PC may sleep. On a still picture
  nobody is touching, the screen dims on the laptop's own timeout. While the
  picture moves (a video, a game), it stays on.
- **Frames the screen can show, no more (Hyper).** A laptop that drops from
  240 Hz to 60 on battery gets 60 frames a second. Older hosts ignore the
  request and keep streaming as before.
- **Fewer copies and passes.** Decoded video is drawn straight from the
  decoder (RDP and Hyper). A Hyper picture shown 1:1 goes to the screen in
  one GPU pass. Where the display has a hardware video overlay (most Intel
  laptops), it goes there instead, and the GPU's 3D engine and Windows'
  compositor stay idle. The log says `video on a hardware overlay plane`
  when that happens. `RDPIO_VIDEO_LAYER=0` turns the overlay off.
- **Fewer wake-ups.** The 1 ms timer is held only while video plays. Small
  Hyper frames on a clean link go without their duplicate error-correction
  packet. Tile decoding uses threads kept between frames instead of new ones
  every frame. Clipboard, file and update work runs as low-priority
  background work.

### Downloads

- `rdpio-windows-x86_64.zip`: rdpio.exe, its signed manifest and the README.
- `rdpio.exe` and `rdpio.exe.update`: what updates download.
- `rdpio-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`: the Linux build (headless, see PORTING.md).
- `*.sha256`: checksums of the archives.
