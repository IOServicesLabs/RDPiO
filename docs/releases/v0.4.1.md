## rdpio 0.4.1

Cloud PCs from the launcher, Teams cameras under SlimCore, and a laptop that
sips battery while it streams.

- **Windows 365 Cloud PCs in the launcher.** Connect to a Cloud PC straight
  from the hub, with COM (serial) port redirection and Teams camera and mic
  for RDP and Cloud PC sessions.
- **Teams under SlimCore (VDI 2.0).** rdpio probes the black-box SlimCore
  plugin so Teams sees the client's cameras without taking the physical
  camera from this PC.
- **Hotkeys on screen.** A stream shows its hotkeys for the first seconds
  (Ctrl+Shift+Q quit, Ctrl+Shift+M mouse capture, Ctrl+Shift+O office/game,
  and the rest); Ctrl+Alt+End sends Ctrl+Alt+Del to the host.
- **Reconnecting always starts fresh.** Reconnecting from the launcher after
  a disconnect begins a clean session instead of resuming a stale one.
- **A longer battery while streaming.** An idle stream on battery hands back
  its keep-awake and Wi-Fi holds so the laptop may sleep, and office mode on
  battery caps the stream at 60 fps and batches pointer motion — game mode
  is never touched.
- **Back from sleep or hibernation.** The stream shows "Reconnecting…" and
  signs in afresh instead of sitting frozen on a stale link.
- **Take the host back.** Ctrl+Alt+F12 at the host ends the stream and hands
  the PC back to its own keyboard and screen.
