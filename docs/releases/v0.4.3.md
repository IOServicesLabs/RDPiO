## rdpio 0.4.3

- **Turn off the bar at the top of a session.** If you go by the hotkeys,
  Settings → Shortcuts → "Show the bar at the top of a full-screen session"
  removes the Pin, Minimize, Window, Stats, Save and Disconnect bar from
  sessions you start afterwards, Remote Desktop and Hyper alike. It stays on
  unless you turn it off. Outside the launcher, set `RDPIO_NO_SESSION_BAR=1`
  for the session.

Everything in [0.4.2](v0.4.2.md) is included.
