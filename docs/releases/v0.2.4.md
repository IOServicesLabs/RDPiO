## rdpio 0.2.4

Sessions side by side in tabs, everything rdpio logs in its window, the
laptop's camera on the PC it streams from, and fixes for black windows after
hibernation.

### Workspace and Activity

- **Workspace (new page).** Saved connections in folders ("Work/Servers"
  nests; right-click, or drag connections onto a folder), and sessions open
  side by side in tabs inside the rdpio window. Ctrl+Tab switches, Ctrl+W or
  a middle-click disconnects, **Pop out** gives a session a window of its
  own. A tab that isn't in front pauses its picture. Each tab has its own
  log, and one that doesn't connect stays open with the reason and a
  **Reconnect** button. Closing rdpio pops the sessions out instead of
  ending them.
- **Activity (new page).** What rdpio and every session it started are
  logging, live: filter by session, problems only, or text; copy it; open
  the log files. The "Connecting…" notice shows the session's latest step,
  and a failed connection says why (with **Show the log**).

### Hibernation

- **No more black windows after a resume.** A lost "screen on" or
  "unlocked" notification no longer keeps a session's picture paused: a
  key, a click or the window's focus brings it back, and a resume clears it.
- **The rdpio window recovers.** After a resume it redraws itself, and if
  its web view loses a process it reloads or restarts instead of staying
  black.
- **A Hyper stream with no picture says so** ("waiting for its picture")
  instead of showing black.

### Hyper

- **The laptop's camera on the host.** On a Windows 11 host it appears to
  apps as **rdpio Camera**; the laptop's camera, and its light, are on only
  while an app on the host uses it. Both ends need 0.2.4 (the host updates
  itself; a laptop is updated by the host when it connects).
  `RDPIO_HYPER_CAMERA=0` on the laptop keeps its camera to itself.
- **The microphone, explained.** When an app on the host records from a
  microphone other than the stream's (Discord set to a particular one, say),
  the laptop shows a notice naming the app: set it to "Default".

### Battery

- **Fewer wake-ups.** Audio capture (the laptop's microphone, the host's
  game audio) wakes when Windows has sound ready instead of polling; RDP
  microphone and camera data wake the session as it arrives; the host hears
  an app start recording at once instead of checking three times a second;
  Teams and the redirector sleep until there's work; fast mice send at most
  one packet a millisecond.
- **Teams call video is fitted on the GPU** (scaled, cropped, mirrored and
  converted in one pass) instead of pixel by pixel on the CPU.
- **The host converts only the frames it sends** from a virtual display.
- **The rdpio window rests while hidden** (and its animations while another
  window is in front).

### Downloads

- `rdpio-windows-x86_64.zip`: rdpio.exe, its signed manifest and the README.
- `rdpio.exe` and `rdpio.exe.update`: what updates download.
- `rdpio-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`: the Linux build (headless, see PORTING.md).
- `*.sha256`: checksums of the archives.
