## rdpio 0.2.5

Less work for the same picture: the camera, audio and stream all cost less,
the stream fits the window it's shown in, Ctrl+Shift+F switches between full
screen and a window, and Connect works again after hibernation without
restarting rdpio.

### Hibernation

- **Connect opens a session after a resume.** If a session's window was gone
  but its process wasn't (stuck closing across a hibernation), Connect tried
  to bring that session forward and nothing opened until rdpio was
  restarted. Now it ends that session and starts a new one, and a closed
  Hyper session exits within 10 seconds at most.
- **The rdpio window keeps a log file** (`logs\launcher.log`, the previous
  run as `.prev`), so what happened before a restart is still there.

### Hyper

- **Ctrl+Shift+F: full screen or a window**, on the screen the window is on.
- **The stream fits the window.** In a window smaller than the laptop's
  screen (a Workspace tab, a restored window) the host streams at the
  window's size a moment after it stops changing, and back up as it grows
  (a 1200x800 window: 5.7 -> 2.2 Mbps).
- **The camera is compressed on the graphics chip** (Quick Sync on an Intel
  laptop) instead of in software: about a fifth of the processor time.
  `RDPIO_CAMERA_CPU=1` forces the software encoder.
- **Fewer audio packets.** The microphone goes in 20 ms packets and sends
  next to nothing between words; in office mode game sound goes in 20 ms
  packets too; the copy of the previous packet is sent only after a loss.
  About half the packets; with a 0.2.4 peer, 10 ms packets as before.
- **Less bitrate on a still desktop.** No picture is coded finer than the
  eye can tell (quantizer 12): a desktop with a little motion 4.3 -> 1.0
  Mbps. `RDPIO_NVENC_MINQP=n` on the host sets another floor (0: none).
- **Steady encoder speed on the host.** While a stream runs, the host's
  NVIDIA GPU is kept at 1500 MHz or more instead of dropping to idle clocks,
  where each frame took several milliseconds longer
  (`RDPIO_GPU_CLOCK_FLOOR=MHz`, 0 to turn off).
- **Half the status packets** from the laptop while nothing is lost.

### RDP

- **The camera** is compressed on the graphics chip there too.
- **RDP over UDP** wakes when data arrives instead of every 15 ms.

### Downloads

- `rdpio-windows-x86_64.zip`: rdpio.exe, its signed manifest and the README.
- `rdpio.exe` and `rdpio.exe.update`: what updates download.
- `rdpio-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`: the Linux build (headless, see PORTING.md).
- `*.sha256`: checksums of the archives.
